Sustainable food-service cutlery

Plastic-free cutlery, built for real kitchens.

Bbeta makes biodegradable bamboo spoons, forks, knives and chopsticks for canteens, restaurants and cloud kitchens who need a durable plastic alternative they can order in bulk — not just a retail eco-product.

Why it matters

Responsible consumption

SDG 13

Cuts the plastic waste and emissions tied to producing and disposing of single-use cutlery.

SDG 15

Life on land

Reduces plastic pollution reaching soil and waterways, while supporting bamboo cultivation.
